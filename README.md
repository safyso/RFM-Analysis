# Customer Segmentation using RFM Analysis

## Project Overview
This project implements an end-to-end customer segmentation analysis for a retail superstore using the **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** model. The goal is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and group them into actionable segments to optimize marketing strategies, improve customer retention, and maximize ROI.

## Methodology
The RFM model evaluates customers based on three behavioral dimensions derived from their transaction history:
1. **Recency (R):** Days since the customer's last purchase.
2. **Frequency (F):** Total number of distinct orders placed.
3. **Monetary (M):** Total revenue generated by the customer.

Using **Quintile Scoring**, customers were assigned scores from 1 to 5 for each dimension, resulting in a combined RFM score. Based on these scores, customers were categorized into 9 distinct segments (e.g., Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, Lost/Hibernating).

## Key Business Insights
* **Core Purchasing Power (Champions & Loyal Customers):** Identified 106 "Champions" (Avg Spend: $5,287, Avg Frequency: 9.3) and 159 "Loyal Customers" (Avg Spend: $3,599). These segments drive the majority of revenue and should be targeted with exclusive loyalty programs.
* **Churn Prevention (At Risk):** Detected 101 high-value customers (Avg Spend: $4,411) who have not purchased in ~222 days. This highlights a critical need for targeted win-back campaigns to recover potential lost revenue.
* **Growth Opportunities (Promising / New Customers):** Found 89 customers with very recent purchases (Avg Recency: 26.5 days). They present a prime opportunity for upselling and cross-selling.
* **Marketing Budget Optimization (Lost / Hibernating):** Segregated 117 customers with low spend ($794) and high inactivity (386 days). Marketing efforts toward this segment can be minimized to reduce wasted ad spend.
